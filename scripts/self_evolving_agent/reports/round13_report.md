# Round 1 -> round 3 eval, score vs. interactivity (both envs, with baselines)

Same underlying batch and data as `reports/draft_round135_v2.md` (`run_repeat_batch_round135_allgpu.sh`,
2026-09-12/13, post best-score-regression-prompting fix; same corrected `_load_valid_rows()`
valid-round definition — crashed / compile-failed / no-fresh-`act()` rounds excluded), restricted
here to just the first two checkpoints (round 1 and round 3), dropping round 5. x-axis is act()
calls/minute (log scale), y-axis is score (avg_reward, log scale), both fixed baselines
(no_reasoning / reasoning) plotted as gray diamonds. Plots: `reports/doom_round13_scatter.png`,
`reports/health_gathering_round13_scatter.png`. Generated by `generate_round13_report.py`.

## doom

| variant | round 1 (score @ rate) | round 3 (score @ rate) | baseline no_reasoning | baseline reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| detailed (n=1 of 2) | 6.6 @ 11.5/min | 3.6 @ 27.6/min | 4.15 | 4.85 |
| minimal (n=3) | 2.9 @ 20.2/min | **7.5** @ 16.5/min | 4.15 | 4.85 |

- **minimal**: round 1 starts *below* both baselines (2.9 — two of three reps crashed inside
  `act()` every episode that round, scoring a real 0.0), then jumps to 7.5 by round 3, clearing
  both baselines with interactivity (~16-20 act()/min) staying in the real-inference range
  throughout. Clean win over this window.
- **detailed**: only 1 of 2 usable reps contributes any point (the other's entire history is
  fallback-latency and gets excluded by the outlier filter). That one rep starts above baseline at
  round 1 (6.6) but regresses to 3.6 by round 3, below both baselines. Treat as inconclusive —
  n=1.

## health_gathering

| variant | round 1 (score @ rate) | round 3 (score @ rate) | baseline no_reasoning | baseline reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| detailed (n=3 / n=2) | 551 @ 9.9/min | **1404** @ 20.6/min | 405.6 | 360.8 |
| minimal (n=3 / n=2) | 95 @ 9.8/min | 358 @ 142/min* | 405.6 | 360.8 |

\* minimal round 3's rate is a mean over 2 reps, one of which sits at ~267 act()/min (fast but
under the 10,000/min outlier cutoff) — flagged as borderline-fast, not excluded.

- **detailed**: round 1 (551) already clears both baselines; round 3 (1404) roughly triples it —
  the strongest round-1->round-3 gain of any cell in this window.
- **minimal**: round 1 (95) sits well below both baselines (two of three reps crashed inside
  `act()` every episode, scoring 0.0); round 3 (358) recovers to just under the no_reasoning
  baseline (405.6) and just under reasoning (360.8) — real improvement, but not yet a baseline
  beat within this window.

## Takeaway

Within round 1 -> round 3 alone, three of the four cells show genuine, real-inference-backed
improvement (doom/minimal, health_gathering/detailed, health_gathering/minimal), and two clear
baseline beats (doom/minimal, health_gathering/detailed). doom/detailed is the exception, but its
result rests on a single usable rep and shouldn't be read as a confirmed regression. This view
doesn't capture the round-3->round-5 decay seen in both health_gathering variants in the full
round-1/3/5 report — see `reports/draft_round135_v2.md` for that.
